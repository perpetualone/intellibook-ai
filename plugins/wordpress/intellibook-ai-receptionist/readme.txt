The official WordPress plugin for Intellibook AI (https://www.intellibookai.com). Connect your salon, clinic, spa or fitness studio with 24/7 autonomous WhatsApp appointment scheduling.

== Description ==

[Intellibook AI](https://www.intellibookai.com) is a 24/7 WhatsApp AI receptionist and appointment booking platform for service-based businesses:
* Hair & Beauty Salons
* Dental & Medical Clinics
* Dermatology & Aesthetic Centres
* Spas & Wellness Centres
* Gyms & Yoga Studios

This free WordPress plugin allows you to easily connect your WordPress website to your Intellibook AI account. 

### Key Features:
* **One-Click Floating WhatsApp Button:** Let site visitors book appointments instantly on WhatsApp.
* **Embeddable Booking Page:** Embed your online booking page on any page or post using the shortcode `[intellibook_booking]`.
* **Instant WhatsApp Confirmations:** Customers receive automated booking confirmation messages on WhatsApp.
* **Automated Reminders & Zero No-Shows:** Automated reminders sent 24 hours and 2 hours before appointments, reducing no-shows by up to 70%.
* **UPI Deposit Collection:** Collect booking token amounts or full service fees via UPI and Cashfree.
* **Google Calendar & Multi-Staff Sync:** Syncs bi-directionally with your stylists', doctors', or practitioners' Google Calendars.

Learn more and create your free business account at [https://www.intellibookai.com](https://www.intellibookai.com).

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin files to the `/wp-content/plugins/intellibook-ai-receptionist` directory, or install the plugin through the WordPress plugins screen directly.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.
3. Use the 'Settings' -> 'Intellibook AI' screen to enter your business ID or booking-link name (for example `14` or `14-glow-salon`).
4. Optional: Embed your booking page in any page by adding the `[intellibook_booking]` shortcode.

== External services ==

This plugin shows your booking page hosted by Intellibook AI (https://www.intellibookai.com), either in an embedded frame on pages that use the `[intellibook_booking]` shortcode or through the floating button, which opens it in a new tab. The booking page loads from Intellibook AI's servers, so the visitor's browser connects to Intellibook AI when it is displayed. Intellibook AI terms: https://www.intellibookai.com/terms. Privacy policy: https://www.intellibookai.com/privacy.

An optional "Powered by Intellibook AI" link can be turned on in the plugin settings. It is off by default.
